# Amateur player-profile product recon

Reviewed 2026-09-14 using public product documentation and pages.

Professional profile products commonly lead with identity, a concise bio, headline statistics, results, media, and social links. For this product those patterns were deliberately adapted for amateurs: local room and community context are more prominent than rankings or earnings; unofficial accomplishments are welcomed with provenance; verified totals are limited to linked Pocket Rankings source records.

The profile avoids claims that a community accolade is official. It does not show purses, agents, commercial endorsements, professional status, or scraped ratings. Facebook and X are treated as optional outbound public surfaces rather than identity providers or data sources.
